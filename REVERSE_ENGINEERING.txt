The comparison is the installed/current build versus the required build. The
CINC produces state 2 when current < required and state 1 otherwise. The
forced-update path is therefore neutralized by replacing only the result-forming
instruction at main-image offset 0x155BE8:

  original bytes:    14 A5 88 9A  (cinc x20, x8, lt)
  replacement bytes: F4 03 08 AA  (mov  x20, x8)

The tweak checks the four original bytes first. A different build is not patched.
